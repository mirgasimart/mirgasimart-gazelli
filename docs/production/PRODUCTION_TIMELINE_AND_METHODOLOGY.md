# Production Methodology & Strategic Timelines: 5-Artwork Commission

Executing five monumental and medium-scale mixed-media masterworks (100×150 cm to 150×200 cm) by hand requires strict studio management. Working with disparate geological matter (drying volcanic clay, oxidizing bitumen, moisture-sensitive salt, and heavy oil impasto) demands an **assembly-line batching methodology** rather than painting canvases one by one.

---

## 1. Core Production Methodology: The Parallel Batching System

If a painter works linearly (Canvas 1 → Canvas 2 → Canvas 3 → Canvas 4 → Canvas 5), drying and curing dead-times cause massive delays (over 6–8 months). By contrast, **The Parallel Batching System** divides studio labor into specialized material phases:

```
[Phase 1: Sourcing & Prep] ──▶ All 5 Canvases Stretched & Mineral-Gesso Grounded
                                          │
[Phase 2: Composition]     ──▶ All 5 Muscular Underdrawings & Sgraffito Carvings
                                          │
[Phase 3: Figurative Core] ──▶ Figurative Portrait Painting in 2-Canvas Rotations
                                          │
[Phase 4: Mineral Tectonics]──▶ Volcanic Clay, Bitumen Slabs & Shell Embeddings
                                          │
[Phase 5: Crystallization] ──▶ Masazir Salt Embedding, Retouching & Archival Varnish
```

### Key Efficiency Multipliers:
1. **Single Expedited Sourcing Run (Days 1–3)**: Gather all 8 Absheron materials in one coordinated 2-day excursion across the peninsula (Garadagh, Masazir, Lokbatan, Binagadi, Bilgah). Sift, sterilize, and catalog in airtight studio bins before opening a single tube of paint.
2. **Batch Grounding (Days 4–6)**: Mix a large 15-liter vat of Absheron Mineral Gesso Ground (Recipe A). Coat all five canvases on the same weekend. They cure simultaneously under studio fans.
3. **Rotational Portrait Painting**: While oil layers on Canvas 1 & 2 are setting to touch (48 hours), execute the figurative portraits on Canvas 3 & 4.
4. **Mineral Curing Window**: Apply mud-volcano craquelure and bitumen early so they have weeks to settle and cure before final varnishing.

## 2. Schedule Option A: The 2-Month Accelerated Sprint (8 Weeks)

*Designed for tight exhibition deadlines, requiring 35–40 studio hours per week.*

| Week | Phase Focus | Key Deliverables & Milestones | Quality Control Checkpoint |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Week 2** | **Underdrawing & Sgraffito Architectural Geometry** | - Transfer life-size cartoons/drawings of the 5 muses and animals.<br>- Carve incised sgraffito geometric lineaments across all 5 canvases.<br>- Lay in warm monochrome tonal underpainting (burnt umber / raw sienna). | **QC 2**: Verify accurate proportions and dynamic eye contact of muses. |
| **Week 3** | **Figurative Painting: Group A (Canvases 1, 2, 4)** | - Full-color figurative execution of Muse + Seal (Canvas 1).<br>- Profile Muse + Didacna hands (Canvas 2).<br>- Reclining Muse + Goby (Canvas 4). | **QC 3**: Ensure skin tones have signature translucency and dark brows are sharp. |
| **Week 4** | **Figurative Painting: Group B (Canvases 3, 5)** | - Double-faced Muse + Shad (Canvas 3).<br>- Frontal Muse + Coiled Lamprey (Canvas 5).<br>- Touch up and glaze figures on Canvases 1, 2, 4. | **QC 4**: Check harmonic continuity across all 5 portraits. |
| **Week 5** | **Heavy Mineral & Tectonic Application** | - Apply Lokbatan volcanic mud craquelure to Canvas 4.<br>- Trowel Binagadi bitumen slabs and embed pyrite onto Canvas 3.<br>- Embed fossil Didacna shells and coquina stone into Canvas 2. | **QC 5**: Verify structural adhesion of shells and stone fragments under shear stress. |
| **Week 6** | **Masazir Salt Crystallization & Textural Integration** | - Embed Masazir pink salt crystal clusters onto Canvas 1 and Canvas 5.<br>- Refine drapery, backgrounds, and palette-knife impasto across all works. | **QC 6**: Check crystal facet sparkle and verify zero salt shedding. |
| **Week 7** | **Studio Curing & Final Detail Glazes** | - Low-humidity dehumidification and air circulation to cure mud and oil.<br>- Final delicate glaze adjustments to eyes, lips, and marine scales.<br>- Clean canvas edges and paint borders. | **QC 7**: Complete photographic review of color balance and surface sheen. |
| **Week 8** | **Archival Sealing, Framing & Documentation** | - Apply 2 mist coats of UV-resistant archival mineral fixative/varnish.<br>- High-resolution studio photography for catalog and press.<br>- Professional crating and preparation for gallery handover. | **QC 8**: Final archival sign-off; exhibition-ready. |

## 3. Schedule Option B: The 4-Month Master Cycle (16 Weeks)

*The ideal fine-art schedule allowing deep contemplation, multi-stage oil glazing, and natural drying kinetics.*

| Month | Core Focus & Milestones | Strategic Advantages |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Month 1**<br>*(Weeks 1–4)* | **Field Expeditions, Material Curation & Structural Foundations**<br>- Comprehensive geological field collection and laboratory-style sorting.<br>- Custom fabrication of museum-grade 45mm deep cedar/aluminum stretchers.<br>- Multi-tiered textured grounds with natural stone aggregate and coquina bedding.<br>- Full-size cartoon preparatory studies on paper. | No rushing material gathering; perfect chemical curing of mineral grounds without cracking. |
| **Month 2**<br>*(Weeks 5–8)* | **Master Portraiture & Anatomical Realization**<br>- Deep, slow-drying oil layers on the faces, dark connected brows, and sculpted hands.<br>- Complex anatomical modeling of Caspian fauna (seal whiskers, fish scales, lamprey suction disc).<br>- Rich sculptural drapery rendering in contemporary haute-couture folds. | Allows classical "fat-over-lean" oil layering, preventing premature skinning or yellowing. |
| **Month 3**<br>*(Weeks 9–12)* | **Geological Integration & Tectonic Curing**<br>- Application of thick Lokbatan mud-volcano craquelure (cured naturally over 14 days).<br>- Heavy impasto bitumen knifing with Dammar and stand oil glazes.<br>- Deep relief embedding of real Didacna fossils and pyrite sulfide crystals.<br>- Fine sgraffito carving into semi-cured paint surfaces. | Maximum tactile depth; natural craquelure develops authentic micro-fissures impossible to fake. |
| **Month 4**<br>*(Weeks 13–16)* | **Masazir Salt Crystallization, Archival Chemistry & Handover**<br>- Precision salt crystal bed setting and multi-stage moisture isolation sealing.<br>- Luminous transparent oil glazes bringing depth to shadows and backgrounds.<br>- Full 3-week studio drying and degasification.<br>- Archival isolation coats, UV satin varnishing, bespoke float-framing, and catalog photography. | Zero risk of moisture entrapment under salt; archival museum permanence for 100+ years. |

## 4. Key Quality Assurance Rules for Mirgasim's Studio

1. **Moisture Isolation on Masazir Salt**: Never apply water-based acrylic mediums over salt once it has crystallized. Always seal with mineral solvent-based fixatives or spray varnishes.
2. **Weight Distribution on Canvases**: Because Canvases 2 and 4 carry heavy coquina and mud, reinforce stretchers with cross-bars and double-staple the linen edges.
3. **No Carpet Clichés**: Ensure garments remain architectural, draped, or sleek contemporary textiles. Let the emotional intimacy between the muse and the animal take center stage.
4. **Signature Continuity**: Maintain the recognizable dark connected eyebrow, statuesque gaze, and elongated fingers across all 5 canvases so they are unmistakably recognized as Mirgasim's masterworks.
