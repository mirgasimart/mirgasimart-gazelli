# Local Raw Materials & Concrete Supplier Sourcing Guide
## Baku & Absheron Peninsula Geological Procurement Manual

This guide answers a critical question for Mirgasim's studio: **Which raw Absheron materials can be sourced effortlessly from local concrete and stone suppliers in Baku, and which materials must be personally gathered from the wild?**

---

Commercial table salt lacks the natural pink beta-carotene tint and large, chunky crystal facets. |

## 2. Directory of Baku Concrete Suppliers & Stone Markets (*Daş Bazarları*)

If you choose to procure bulk limestone dust, aggregate, or sand commercially, visit these primary hubs in Baku:

### 1. Dərnəgül Tikinti və Daş Bazarı (Dernegul Building Market)
- **Location**: Ziya Bünyadov prospekti, Dərnəgül, Baku.
- **What to Ask For**:
  - *Əhəng tozu* (Limestone powder / lime flour) — sold in white 25 kg woven sacks.
  - *Gülbax yonqar tozu* (Fine dust from soft Absheron freestone).
  - *Kvars dəniz qumu* (Washed quartz sand).
- **Pricing**: ₼5.00–₼7.00 per 25 kg sack.

### 2. Binəqədi Daş Kəsmə Sexləri (Binaqadi Stone-Cutting Workshops)
- **Location**: Binəqədi şosesi (along the road leading towards Mehdiabad/Novxanı).
- **What to Ask For**:
  - Visit any outdoor stone-cutting workshop (*daş sexi*) cutting Absheron masonry blocks (*Ağlay*).
  - Ask the master (*Usta*): *"Gülbax daşının xırda tozundan və kəsim qırıntısından götürmək olar?"* (Can I collect fine cutting dust and offcuts?).
  - They will often let you fill 2–3 buckets for free or for ₼5–₼10 pocket money.

### 3. Şüvəlan & Türkan Coastal Quarries (Coquina Specialists)
- **Location**: Eastern Absheron Peninsula (Turkan / Shuvalan coastal roads).
- **What to Ask For**:
  - *Balıqqulağı daşı qırıntısı* (Crushed shell-stone coquina).
  - Ask for porous pieces with intact, visible fossilized marine shell casts.

## 3. The 100% Artist Wild Harvesting Itinerary (2-Day Expedition)

For maximum artistic authenticity and conceptual purity, the artist can gather all signature substances directly from nature over two dedicated field days using a rented 4WD vehicle:

```
DAY 1: NORTH-WEST & SOUTH-WEST EXPEDITION (Lake & Volcano)
  09:00 ──▶ Depart Baku Studio
  09:45 ──▶ Stop 1: Masazır Pink Salt Lake (Eastern shoreline)
            Harvest: 10 kg unrefined pink salt crust & crystal clusters
  12:30 ──▶ Drive southwest via Baku Ring Road to Lokbatan
  13:15 ──▶ Stop 2: Lökbatan Mud Volcano (Active gryphon craters)
            Harvest: Two 10L buckets of fresh extruded volcanic mud + dry craquelure slabs
  16:30 ──▶ Return to Studio & de-sand samples

DAY 2: CENTRAL BITUMEN & EASTERN CASPIAN COAST
  09:30 ──▶ Stop 3: Binaqədi Prehistoric Asphaltite Lake (Binaqədi qır gölü)
            Harvest: 5 kg pure surface bitumen seep
  12:00 ──▶ Drive east along the Absheron Peninsula highway to Türkan / Bilgəh
  13:00 ──▶ Stop 4: Coastal Beach Wrackline (Türkan / Pirşağı dunes)
            Harvest: Intact Didacna bivalve cockle shells & pure dune sand
  16:00 ──▶ Return to Studio & begin chemical sterilization
```

### Harvest Checklist & Gear Required

- **Transport**: Six 10-liter heavy-duty plastic airtight buckets with snap-on lids.
- **Tools**: Hand trowel, stainless steel masonry spatula, heavy geological hammer.
- **Safety**: Heavy-duty rubber/nitrile gloves (bitumen is extremely sticky; mud gryphons can be slippery), durable hiking boots, sunglasses (high glare off white Masazir salt flats).
- **Solvent for Cleanup**: A small bottle of vegetable oil or odorless mineral spirits to clean bitumen off tools and hands on site.

## 4. Studio Preparation Protocols (From Field to Canvas)

Following field collection, raw substances must undergo the archival stabilization protocols defined in [`docs/production/ARTIST_STUDIO_MANUAL.md`](../production/ARTIST_STUDIO_MANUAL.md):

1. **Masazir Salt**:
   - Do **NOT** wash with water (it will dissolve).
   - Bake at 60°C in a low oven for 2 hours to dehydrate ambient moisture.
   - Bed into Amsterdam Extra Heavy Gloss Gel; seal with Talens UV Matt Varnish spray.
2. **Didacna Shells**:
   - Soak in 5% hydrogen peroxide or mild household bleach for 2 hours to whiten and eliminate marine odor.
   - Air dry thoroughly before embedding.
3. **Mud-Volcano Clay**:
   - Sift wet mud through a 2 mm wire kitchen sieve to remove sharp rocks.
   - Blend 60% clay with 25% Amsterdam Modeling Paste and 15% water to achieve controlled, archival craquelure.
4. **Binagadi Bitumen**:
   - Dissolve raw asphaltite chunks in odorless white spirit until reaching a molasses consistency.
   - Mix 50/50 with Amsterdam Gloss Gel or Talens Stand Oil for permanent, non-tacky drying.
