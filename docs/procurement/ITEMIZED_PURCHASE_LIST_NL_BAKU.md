## 6. International Cargo Shipping Instructions (NL → Baku)

- **Total Consignment Weight**: ~55–60 kg
- **Recommended Cargo Carriers**:
  - **Camex / Limak / Starex NL Drop-off**: Azerbaijani forwarding services operating receiving addresses in Europe (often charge ~€3.50–€4.50 per kg).
  - **Silk Way West Airlines Cargo / DHL Express Freight**: Direct Amsterdam Schiphol (AMS) to Baku Heydar Aliyev Airport (GYD).
- **Packaging Protocol**:
  1. **Modular Stretchers & Canvas Roll**: Strap the stretcher timber pairs along the 2.10m rigid cardboard canvas tube. Wrap in heavy industrial stretch wrap.
  2. **Liquids & Gels**: Seal all screw-top lids of gesso and gel tubs with duct tape. Place inside heavy ziplock bags to withstand air cabin pressure changes.
  3. **Aerosol Sprays (UV Varnish)**: Declare 4 spray cans under non-hazardous limited quantity (LQ) or pack in accordance with carrier air transport guidelines.

## 7. Customs Clearance in Baku (GYD Terminal)

- **Declaration Category**: *Personal Fine Art Materials / Rəssamlıq və Təsviri Sənət Ləvazimatları*.
- **Duty Exemption Threshold**: Azerbaijan customs exempts non-commercial personal goods up to $800 USD (~€735 EUR) per calendar month for individuals via express cargo. Because this consignment is valued at ~€1,155 EUR, standard simplified duty on the excess (~36% of the amount over $800) or personal declaration will incur a modest terminal handling fee of approximately **₼64.75 AZN (€35.00)**, already budgeted in Category E.
