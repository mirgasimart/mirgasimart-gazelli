## 3. Cost-Efficiency Analysis: Why Sourcing in the Netherlands (NL) Wins

1. **Royal Talens Domestic Advantage**: Royal Talens is manufactured in Apeldoorn, Netherlands. Purchasing **Amsterdam Standard Acrylics** and **Van Gogh Oil Colours** via Dutch retail art distributors (Van Beek Art Supplies, Gerstaecker NL, De Kat) eliminates import tariffs, regional distributor markups, and currency arbitrage.
   - *Example*: A 1000 ml tub of Amsterdam Extra Heavy Gel Medium costs **€26.00 (~₼48 AZN)** in the Netherlands versus **₼85–₼100 AZN** when imported via boutique retailers in Baku (when available).
2. **Availability of Knock-Down Modular Stretchers**: Large-format canvases (185×120 cm, 180×180 cm, 120×200 cm) require tongue-and-groove modular bevelled stretcher bars. Sourcing modular pine bars in the Netherlands allows them to be bundled flat into compact cargo boxes, saving hundreds of AZN compared to bespoke Baku carpentry.
3. **Purity of Raw Absheron Geological Matter**: Sourcing limestone dust, volcanic mud, bitumen, and salt locally across Absheron costs practically zero in commercial product cost—the only expense is 4WD vehicle fuel (₼95 AZN) and storage containers (₼33 AZN).
