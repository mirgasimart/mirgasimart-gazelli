# Artist Brief

Develop an artwork inspired by the natural environment of the Caspian Sea and the Absheron Peninsula. Incorporate materials from the list below. The suggested animal species and groups can inform the imagery, forms and textures of your work.

## Material requirements

Every artwork must incorporate at least one material from the list. One listed material is sufficient; several may be combined. These materials may also be used alongside any other artistic materials.

## Materials

| Material | Where it comes from | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coquina<br>(shell limestone) | Absheron Peninsula quarries | A porous limestone made mainly of marine shells and shell fragments. |
| Absheron limestone | Local quarries on the Absheron Peninsula | A porous local limestone and the traditional building stone of Baku and Absheron. |
| Sand | Absheron beaches | Coastal sand mixed with shell fragments. |
| Caspian shells | Absheron shore | Empty natural shells, including Caspian cockles. |
| Salt | Masazir salt lake, Absheron | Crystalline lake salt. The lake water can appear pink. |
| Mud-volcano clay | Mud volcanoes of Absheron and the Caspian region | Clay-rich mud ejected from underground by mud volcanoes. |
| Pyrite | Mud-volcano ejecta, Azerbaijan | A sulfide mineral with a metallic appearance, often found as irregular aggregates. |
| Natural bitumen | Absheron deposits, including Kirmaki, Binagadi and Fatmai | Dark natural hydrocarbon materials, including bitumen and asphaltite. |

## What to submit

Please prepare a brief description of your concept, a sketch, a visual reference board created with the help of AI, a list of selected materials, and an explanation of how they will be used. If your work refers to a specific animal, identify the species or group.
