## 2. Primary Client Hypotheses & Target Customer Profiles

We analyze the client dilemma between two primary profiles:

### Hypothesis 1 (Primary): Gazelli Flagship Wellness Sanctuary / Coastal Resort
- **Client Identity**: Gazelli Group (Dr. Zarifa Hamzayeva) & Gazelli House / Art House (Mila Askarova).
- **Core Philosophy**: Dr. Hamzayeva founded Gazelli in 1999 around the therapeutic, healing, and mineral properties of Absheron mud-volcano clays, thermal spring minerals, and Masazir lake salts.
- **Client Expectations**:
  - Artworks as **holistic, mineral-infused earth-art**: physical extensions of the wellness sanctuary's treatments.
  - Meditative, contemplative atmospheres, clean geological planes, organic craquelure, subtle saline crystalline crusts, and serene marine wildlife (*Pusa caspica*, ancestral Didacna cockles).
  - High sensory and tactile appeal: natural, unrefined, non-toxic, primordial.

### Hypothesis 2 (Secondary): Gazelli Art House as Art Advisory for Luxury Hospitality / Waterfront Landmark
- **Client Identity**: Gazelli Art House acting as lead art consultant for a landmark development (e.g., Crescent Bay / Crescent Hotel Baku, Sea Breeze Resort Baku, or Silk Way / AZAL VIP aviation facilities).
- **Client Expectations**:
  - High-impact, museum-grade visual drama suitable for dramatic double-height lobbies, executive suites, and international VIP reception spaces.
  - Monumental scale, bold architectural framing (shebeke stone tracery, derrick scaffolding, industrial bitumen contrasts with brassy pyrite ore).
  - Engaging dialogue with Azerbaijan’s identity: blending ancient rock petroglyphs (Gobustan), petroleum pioneer history (Binagadi oil wells), and the ecological fragility of the Caspian basin.

## 3. Cultural Heritage: Evolution of the Absheron School
To ensure maximum curatorial resonance, all proposals must position themselves within the authentic lineage of the **Absheron School of Artists**:
1. **The Modernist Predecessors**:
   - Masters like **Javad Mirjavadov, Sattar Bahlulzade, Rasim Babayev, and Tofig Javadov** broke from Soviet Socialist Realism by drawing directly from the raw, wind-beaten terrain of Absheron, folk mythologies, and pagan Zoroastrian fire motifs.
2. **The 21st-Century Evolution**:
   - Replacing conventional paints and nostalgic tourism clichés with the **literal substances of the land** (bitumen, volcanic clay, raw sea salt, and coquina limestone).
   - Rejecting superficial decorative repetition (e.g. plastering every canvas with identical carpet patterns) in favor of **authentic biological accuracy** (Latin scientific taxonomy) and **monumental, museum-grade material integrity**.
3. **Strictly Skip Carpets**:
   - Carpets, kilims, rug borders, and textile fringes are **entirely excluded**. The focus remains uncompromised: raw mineral matter, architectural stone carving (*daş yonma*), Shebeke stone tracery, Gobustan petroglyphs, and Caspian ecology.

## 4. The 5-Artwork Distribution Strategy (Straddling the Dilemma)
When presenting the 5 core deliverables, the portfolio is strategically balanced according to a **3:2 Hybrid Straddle**, tailored for intimate-to-monumental luxury gallery formats (40×60 cm to 185×120 cm):

### Group A: The Mineral Sanctuary (3 Works) — Grounding Hypothesis 1
*Resonates deeply with Gazelli's wellness philosophy of raw geological healing matter:*
1. **Work 1: *The Salt Monoliths of Masazir*** (*Pusa caspica* / Caspian Seal)
   - *Dimensions*: 100 × 150 cm (Portrait)
   - *Materials*: Masazir pink lake salt crystals, Absheron limestone, raw timber lineaments.
   - *Atmosphere*: Serene, meditative geological minimalism; crystalline brine and calm mammal.
2. **Work 2: *The Shebeke Reliquary: Fossil Didacna in Absheron Stone*** (*Didacna* Cockles)
   - *Dimensions*: 40 × 60 cm (Vertical Canvas)
   - *Materials*: Porous coquina quarry stone, beach sand, genuine fossil Didacna bivalve shells.
   - *Atmosphere*: Tactile architectural masonry, ancient stone carving (*daş yonma*), paleontological sea fossils.
3. **Work 3: *Breath of Lokbatan: Volcanic Crater and Armored Goby*** (*Benthophilus* / Caspian Tadpole Goby)
   - *Dimensions*: 120 × 200 cm (Vertical Format)
   - *Materials*: Mud-volcano clay from Lokbatan, natural craquelure fissures, limestone dust.
   - *Atmosphere*: Earth-art vortex, subterranean geothermal forces, prehistoric armored fauna.

### Group B: The Architectural & Geological Horizon (2 Works) — Grounding Hypothesis 2
*Resonates with high-impact corporate, luxury waterfront, and landmark reception spaces:*
4. **Work 4: *The Binagadi Derrick: Hydrocarbon Strata & Pyrite Shad*** (*Alosa braschnikowi* / Marine Shad)
   - *Dimensions*: 120 × 200 cm (Vertical Panoramic Canvas)
   - *Materials*: Natural Binagadi & Fatmai bitumen, metallic pyrite ore, limestone powder.
   - *Atmosphere*: Industrial-geological abstraction, petroleum pioneer history, eternal subterranean flames.
5. **Work 5: *The Gobustan Solar Oracle: Ouroboros Lamprey*** (*Caspiomyzon wagneri* / Caspian Lamprey)
   - *Dimensions*: 100 × 150 cm (Portrait Altar Piece)
   - *Materials*: Concentric beach sand mandala, Masazir salt halos, petroglyphic sgraffito incisions.
   - *Atmosphere*: Ancient mythic cosmology, deep archaeological time, Gobustan petroglyphs carved into stone (no textiles).

## 5. The "Silent Knowledge" Strategy in the Public README
In the public-facing proposal documentation ([docs/output/gazelli_artworks/README.md](file:///Users/jmj/repos/mirgasimart-gazelli/docs/output/gazelli_artworks/README.md)):
- **Never mention** any specific commercial client, development name (Crescent, Sea Breeze, Silk Way), or wellness center.
- Act with complete artistic confidence and curatorial neutrality: present each piece solely with its title, dimensions, materials, species taxonomy, and conceptual narrative.
- The 3:2 internal balance will organically captivate both client profiles without revealing speculative assumptions.
