## *The Anatomy of an Apocalyptic Geological Masterpiece: Black Gold (Qara Qızıl)*

### I. The Conceptual Marriage: Two Visions of Human Folly

To imagine **Francisco Goya (1746–1828)** and **Hieronymus Bosch (c. 1450–1516)** co-authoring Plate 03 on the Absheron Peninsula is to stage the ultimate collision between **grotesque cosmological terror** and **raw, suffocating existential dread**.

This is not an arbitrary pairing. In Azerbaijani art history, **Javad Mirjavadov (1923–1997)**—the spiritual father of the Absheron Modernist School—along with his contemporary **Rasim Babayev (1927–2007)**, spent their entire lives haunted by this exact axis. In Soviet defiance, they fused Bosch’s demonic, purgatorial encyclopedias with Goya’s brutal *Pinturas Negras* (Black Paintings). They realized that the windswept, petroleum-bleeding earth of Absheron was the physical reality both European masters had prophesied.

| Master | Core Philosophy | Signature Dynamic | Painterly Language |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hieronymus Bosch** | **Cosmological Purgatory**: The moral and biological corruption of creation; living flesh and human machines hybridizing into bizarre, tragic instruments of retribution. | **The Encyclopedic Detail & Chimera**: Swarming entanglements of corrupted fauna, hollowed bio-mechanical vessels, and surreal purgatorial architectures. | Glazes of jewel-toned gold, microscopic linear draftsmanship, ominous silhouettes against apocalyptic skies. |
| **Francisco Goya** | **Existential Cannibalism & Sublime Horror**: Time (*Saturn*) devouring its children; blind civilizational hubris swallowed by an indifferent, terrifying earth. | **The Heavy Impasto Void & Emotional Extremes**: Visceral psychological grimaces; suffocating black bitumen; thick palette-knife violence where figures drown in mud. | Slashed bitumen blacks, scorched bone-ash whites, bruised umbers, and dying, sulfurous ember glows. |
| **Absheron School (Mirjavadov & Babayev)** | **Chthonic Shamanism & Petroleum Divs**: The ancient Caspian spirit world; half-beast, half-human earth deities born of oil, sulfur, and ancient petroglyphs. | **The Primal Mask of Awe & Trance**: Massive bulging eyes, ecstatic grimaces, and ancient totems screaming through thick, sculpted earth impasto. | Raw industrial bitumen, volcanic mud craquelure, ground coquina limestone, and pulverized pyrite sand. |

### III. The Core Innovation: Morphed Hybrid Creatures (Inanimate-Animate Synthesis)

The defining leap forward in this masterwork is the total eradication of simplistic "decorative nature." Nature and human machinery have dissolved and hybridized into **living chimeras** (*half-human, half-animal, half-machine, half-concept*):

#### 1. The Prophet-Sturgeon (*Acipenser gueldenstaedtii x Industrial Boiler*)
* **The Hybrid Anatomy**: An ancient 4-meter Caspian sturgeon whose armored dorsal scutes are forged iron shingles held together with heavy industrial rivets. Its ventral belly is hollowed into a glowing combustion chamber where tiny golden embers smolder.
* **The Expression — Elevated Euphoria & Rapture**:
  * Unlike a dead animal, its face is unmistakably semi-human, reminiscent of an ancient Sufi mystic or Goya’s airborne figures in *Asmodea*.
  * Its eyes are turned upward, bathed in gold leaf, radiant with an unearthly, transcendent euphoria. It is not dying; it has transcended physical matter, singing an ecstatic, silent hymn as it is propelled through the petroleum cyclone.

#### 2. The Derrick-Seal (*Pusa caspica x Rusted Rigging*)
* **The Hybrid Anatomy**: The endemic Caspian seal whose streamlined blubber seamlessly transitions into corroded structural steel angle-iron and wire cables. Its flippers have hardened into segmented iron drill augers.
* **The Expression — Paralyzing Bafflement & Vulnerability**:
  * Its face retains the soft, dark, mammalian features of the Caspian seal, but its brow is furrowed in profound, bewildered surprise.
  * It stares directly out of the canvas at the viewer, as if to ask: *"What have you transformed me into?"* It represents the innocent soul of the Caspian caught inside the gearwork of the machine age.

#### 3. The Lantern-Lampreys & Goby Phantasms (*Caspiomyzon wagneri x Brass Gauges*)
* **The Hybrid Anatomy**: Prehistoric, serpentine lampreys whose tooth-ringed suction mouths are fused with circular brass pressure gauges and antique miner's carbide lamps, casting needle-thin beams of sulfurous light across the black canvas.
* **The Expression — Hypnotic Awe (*Stupor Mundi*)**:
  * Swimming alongside are schools of tiny endemic Caspian gobies with wide, porcelain-white human faces.
  * Their mouths form perfect 'O' shapes, frozen in absolute, childlike astonishment and reverence before the towering vortex of black gold.

#### 4. The Geochronological Giants (The Screaming Derricks)
* **The Hybrid Anatomy**: At the center choke-point, the wooden and steel derrick legs are not static carpentry; they are **anthropomorphized into colossi**—Goya’s *Colossus* (*El Coloso*) or Mirjavadov’s primeval *Divs*.
* **The Expression — Primal Terror & Rage**:
  * Knots in the timber and twisted rivet-plates form howling mouths and sunken eye sockets.
  * They writhe as tectonic faults shear through them, screaming in defiance as millions of tons of black earth pull them down into the molten bitumen.

### IV. The Spectrum of the Sublime: Four Emotional States of the Abyss

Why combine terror and euphoria on the same canvas? Because in fine art philosophy, this is the definition of **The Sublime** (*Das Erhabene*), established by Edmund Burke (1757) and Immanuel Kant (1790): *when human consciousness meets something so infinitely massive and catastrophic that dread and ecstasy merge into one overwhelming spiritual shock.*

### V. The Master’s Technique: Physical Alchemical Synthesis

How are these complex hybrid beings rendered without turning into digital illustration or cartoon fantasy? Through **sculptural mineral matter and palette-knife sgraffito**:

1. **The Sculptural Bitumen Bed**:
   * Raw Binagadi asphaltite is heated and troweled onto heavy linen backed with aluminum-cradled birch panel. The bitumen forms deep, sculptural bas-relief ridges (up to 8 mm thick).
2. **Sgraffito Micro-Draftsmanship**:
   * While the tar is semi-cured, surgical steel scalpel tips incise microscopic Boschian details directly into the black substrate: sturgeon gills, clockwork teeth, rivet heads, human eyelashes, and shell growth rings.
3. **Flayed Gold Leaf & Pyrite Encrustation**:
   * 24-karat gold leaf is applied across the creatures' bodies, but then deliberately scuffed, torn, and abraded with steel wool to evoke peeling reptile skin, flaking fish scales, and oxidized gold currency.
4. **Volcanic Mud Craquelure Grounds**:
   * Authentic Lokbatan mud volcano clay dries over organic rabbit-skin glue, shrinking naturally to form genuine geological fissures across the faces of the figures. The cracks become the wrinkles and scars of the chimeras.
