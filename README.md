## 🎨 Curatorial Concept & Modernist Heritage

Responding to the curatorial standards of **Gazelli Art House**, this collection explores the raw geological matter of the Absheron Peninsula and the ancient marine endemism of the Caspian Sea.

Rather than decorative repetitions, the artist delivers an **interlocking 5-movement geological suite**. The work channels the spirit of the **Absheron Modernist School**—masters such as **Javad Mirjavadov, Sattar Bahlulzade, Rasim Babayev, and Tofig Javadov**—who drew directly from Absheron's wind-sculpted terrain, subterranean fires, and mythic coastal presence.

### The Three Evolutionary Pillars:
1. **The Literal Raw Matter of the Land**:  
   Conventional synthetic tube pigments are superseded by the physical matter of Absheron: crystalline pink lake salt from Masazir, Lokbatan mud-volcano clay ejecta, raw unrefined Binagadi bitumen, brassy pyrite ore aggregates, and coastal coquina limestone.
2. **Zero Textile Clichés (Strictly Skip Carpets)**:  
   Carpet borders, kilim patterns, and textile fringes are **entirely excluded**. The aesthetic vocabulary is rooted in *daş yonma* (monumental stone carving), Shebeke architectural stone tracery, Gobustan petroglyphs, and Caspian marine taxonomy.
3. **The 3:2 Curatorial Straddle**:  
   The suite commits to an internal equilibrium: **3 Atmospheric Sanctuary Works** (lacustrine craquelure, salt crystals, and tranquil benthic marine life for wellness and VIP executive suites) and **2 Monumental Horizon Works** (oil derrick scaffolding, eternal subterranean fire, and mud-volcano vortexes for grand double-height atriums).

## 📐 Turnkey Architectural & Engineering Specifications

Every artwork is engineered as an authorial, museum-grade installation eliminating operational ambiguity:

* **Chassis Engineering**: Heavy-duty, kiln-dried Finnish pine stretcher bars cross-braced with aluminum internal perimeter cradles to eliminate torsion and atmospheric humidity warping.
* **Flush Cleat Mounting**: Dual continuous beveled aircraft-grade aluminum **French cleats (Z-bars)** mounted to canvas chassis and wall studs, certified for static loads up to 120 kg.
* **Archival Edging & Framing**:
  * *Standard*: Continuous 45 mm mineral-wrapped gallery edge (painted continuous with coquina primer).
  * *Turnkey Floater*: Recessed matte dark walnut or blackened steel shadow-line floater frame (10 mm air reveal cavity).
* **Conservation & Lightfastness**: Finished with archival, non-yellowing mineral wax and matte micro-crystalline UV barrier (Class 1 lightfastness rating, 100+ year durability).
* **Tactile Material Validation**: Physical *Curatorial Material Palette Box* (cured 15 × 15 cm mineral plaques of lake salt, bitumen, and volcanic clay) available on request for panel tactile inspection.

## 💻 Tech Stack & Web Application Architecture

The interactive virtual gallery is built using zero-dependency, ultra-performant modern web technologies:

* **Structure**: Semantic HTML5 with accessibility landmark roles.
* **Styling**: Vanilla CSS3 using custom CSS properties, flexbox, CSS Grid, container queries, backdrop filters, and fluid `clamp()` sizing.
* **Logic**: Vanilla ES6+ object-oriented class architecture ([`gallery.js`](assets/js/gallery.js)).
* **Responsiveness**: Fully responsive across mobile smartphones (iPhone SE, Pro, Android), tablets (iPad Air, Pro), laptops (13"–16"), desktops (1080p, 1440p), and 4K ultrawide displays (21:9 up to 3840px+).
* **Audio**: Procedural Web Audio API sound generator (zero external MP3/WAV dependencies).
