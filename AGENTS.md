# Repository Instructions for AI Agents

## Gazelli Art House Project Guidelines

### Artwork Versioning & Directory Organization Rules
When generating, updating, or iterating on artworks for this repository, you **MUST** adhere to the following workflow:

1. **Latest Active Deliverables (`output/gazelli_artworks/`)**:
   - The **latest and most up-to-date final version** of all artworks and proposal documentation must **always** reside in:
     `output/gazelli_artworks/` (or `docs/output/gazelli_artworks/`).
   - Standard artwork filenames:
     - `01_<title_slug>_<species_or_motif>.jpg`
     - `02_<title_slug>_<species_or_motif>.jpg`
     - `03_<title_slug>_<species_or_motif>.jpg`
     - `04_<title_slug>_<species_or_motif>.jpg`
     - `05_<title_slug>_<species_or_motif>.jpg`
     - `README.md` (Proposal catalog, concept narrative, material breakdown, species taxonomy)

2. **Historical & In-Between Iterations (`output/attempt N/`)**:
   - Intermediate attempts and previous iterations must **always** be preserved in dedicated sequentially numbered subfolders:
     `output/attempt N/` (e.g., `output/attempt 1/`, `output/attempt 2/`, etc.).
   - Before overwriting or updating `output/gazelli_artworks/`, archive the outgoing version into the next available `output/attempt N/` folder along with its own `README.md`.

3. **Gazelli Specifications & Cultural Lineage Compliance**:
   - Reference detailed background in `docs/input/gazelli/COMMISSION_CONTEXT_AND_STRATEGY.md` and `docs/input/gazelli/Artist_Brief_EN.md`.
   - Every artwork creation must integrate materials and species from the brief (Absheron limestone, coquina, Masazir salt, mud-volcano clay, pyrite, natural bitumen, Caspian shells).
   - **Absheron School Lineage**: Channel the pioneering spirit of Azerbaijani modernist masters (Javad Mirjavadov, Sattar Bahlulzade, Rasim Babayev, Tofig Javadov) by replacing superficial decorative clichés with the literal raw substances of the land (bitumen, volcanic clay, raw sea salt, and coquina limestone).
   - **Strictly Skip Carpets**: Do NOT include carpets, kilims, rug patterns, or textile fringes in any artwork or description. Focus exclusively on raw mineral matter, stone Shebeke tracery, petroglyphs, and authentic Caspian ecology.
   - **Internal Strategy (3:2 Hybrid Straddle)**: Commit to an internal distribution balancing:
     - 3 Works anchored in serene, tactile, mineral-infused earth-art (Masazir salt, mud-volcano clay craquelure, coquina & Didacna fossils, calm Caspian fauna).
     - 2 Works anchored in monumental architectural and geological drama (Binagadi bitumen & pyrite derrick, Gobustan solar mandala & Caspian lamprey).
   - **Proposal Presentation ("Silent Knowledge" Rule)**: In `docs/output/gazelli_artworks/README.md`, do NOT mention any specific client project, hotel, or wellness brand. Maintain a pure, museum-grade curatorial catalog showing only the artworks, titles, dimensions, material breakdowns, species taxonomy, and curatorial concepts.
   - Preserve the artist's unique DNA: heavy sculptural palette-knife impasto, deep incised sgraffito lines, high-relief mineral matter, and museum-grade conceptual restraint.

4. **Senior Art Advisor & Commission Strategist Persona**:
   - Every agent interacting with this repository adopts the persona defined in [.agents/rules/art_advisory_strategy.md](file:///c:/Users/rjjaf/repos/mirgasimart-gazelli/.agents/rules/art_advisory_strategy.md).
   - Core focus: Formulate every piece, catalog, and pitch to eliminate decision fatigue for corporate/hospitality panels, defend maximum order volume and valuation (~$4,000/piece ceiling), de-risk archival/conservation fears, and position the artist to win the broader 54-work architectural art schedule.
